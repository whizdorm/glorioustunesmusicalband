About Glorious Tunes

Discover the story, mission, and people behind GTMB — a family of ex-NYSC band members united by music and purpose.

Our Journey

A Decade of Harmony

Glorious Tunes Musical Band (GTMB) was born out of the shared passion of ex-members of the NYSC Parade Band, Osun State. For over ten years, we have come together to keep the spirit of music alive, fostering unity and excellence among our members.

Although our roots run deep, GTMB was officially registered as an association just two years ago, solidifying our commitment to structure, growth, and a lasting legacy. Today, we continue to perform, train, and inspire each other and our community.

Our journey is a testament to the enduring power of music and the bonds formed through shared experience. We invite you to be part of our story.

Membership

Who We Welcome

GTMB is open to all who share our passion, with clear categories and responsibilities.

🎯 Regular Members
Ex-Banders from NYSC Parade Band, Osun, who possess maturity, musical talent, and shared interest in GTMB's objectives.
🤝 Associate Members
Individuals who support the band but may not meet all regular membership criteria.
🏅 Honorary Members
Conferred upon individuals who have demonstrated exceptional support, commitment, or contribution to GTMB (Article 2.4).

Member Responsibilities: Attend rehearsals and performances, maintain discipline and professionalism, and uphold the image of GTMB.

Honorary Limitations: No voting rights, excluded from sensitive discussions, restricted participation in certain activities, and no holding of executive positions.

Organization

Our Leadership

GTMB is governed by a clear structure that ensures accountability and strategic direction.

🏛️

Board of Directors (BOD)

Composition: 5–7 members including Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer.
Role: Strategic direction, oversight, financial accountability.
Term: 2-year staggered terms, elected by regular members.

⚙️

Executive Committee (EC)

Composition: President, Vice-President, Musical Director, Committee Chairs.
Role: Implement BOD decisions, manage daily operations, coordinate committees.
Term: 1-year terms, elected by regular members, approved by BOD.

📋

Committees

Standing: Finance, Programs, Public Relations, Training & Development.
Ad-Hoc: Formed as needed for special projects.
Role: Execute specific tasks and support EC and BOD.
